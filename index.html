How we choose our picks

We don’t rely on our own timed tests or lab measurements. Instead, our recommendations are built from months of reading owner forums, analyzing long-term reviews on retailer sites, and cross-referencing published expert assessments from trusted woodworking magazines and YouTube channels. We look for patterns in what breaks (switch failures, pad wobble, dust port cracks) and what owners consistently praise (smooth start, low vibration, easy pad changes, good dust collection). A sander earns a top spot only when it has a strong track record across hundreds of owner reports, not just a few glowing first-impression reviews. We also consider availability of replacement parts and pads, because a sander that’s hard to maintain isn’t a long-term keeper. No brand gets special treatment; we only recommend tools that the community and experts agree are genuinely reliable and effective.
